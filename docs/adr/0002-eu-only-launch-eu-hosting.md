# EU/EEA-only launch, EU-hosted

Launch to EU/EEA Users only, with all hosting and sub-processors in the EU (or covered by an adequacy decision / SCCs). This keeps the legal surface to GDPR, Spanish consumer law and LSSI-CE, and EU VAT via a merchant of record, instead of adding CCPA and US state law. Widening later is additive; narrowing after launch is not.
